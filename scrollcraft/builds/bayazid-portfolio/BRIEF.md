# BRIEF — Bayazid HS Portfolio
Self-authored, not interviewed.

## Interview answers

1. Vibe: obsessive builder, black and crimson, terminal-born, playful. References: a workshop at 2am, a locked door with a key you have to find, a terminal that talks back.

2. Scroll journey: the visitor lands on my face and my terminal, scrolls into a vault of 42 certificates, then into a room of 10 project films, then into the campus where I study, then into what I can actually do, then leaves with one way to reach me.

3. Energy curve: the personal intro is calm. The certificate vault is intense. The project films are obsessive. The RUET gallery is calm again. The skills matrix is intense. The contact close is resolve.

4. Feelings per stage and the peak:
   - Curiosity: the typing terminal and the person behind it.
   - Awe: 42 certificates, each one a door opened.
   - Obsession: 10 project films, the actual building.
   - Intimacy: RUET campus, where the journey happens.
   - Confidence: skills matrix, what I can actually do.
   - Resolve: one action, connect.

   Peak: "the screen where all 42 certificates stop being numbers and start being doors, and you realize every one of them was opened by the same person who built the OS."

5. Signature move seed: the site should feel like a puzzle, one room after another, like someone forgot the way out. You solve small gates to enter each room.

6. Aesthetic range: playful. Game-like elements, a puzzle to solve before entering, the visitor earning the next room.

7. Structure: distinct scenes, chapters, cuts. Each room is its own place. Not one continuous flight.

8. Assets already owned: 42 certificate images (real scans), RUET campus photos (Wikimedia Commons), 10 YouTube project videos (placeholders ready), personal memory photos (4 images in assets/images/), brand palette (black, crimson, off-white), monospace + serif + grotesk type stack.
